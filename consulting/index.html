Consulting projects

In addition to our academic and research activities, DSLAB also carries out consultancy projects commissioned by clients seeking data-driven solutions to specific challenges. These projects often involve the design and development of custom tools, data integration pipelines, dashboards, or analytical systems tailored to client needs. Below is a sample of consultancy work we've delivered —⁠spanning sectors such as energy, environmental policy, and digital transformation⁠— illustrating how we apply our technical expertise to generate actionable insights and practical results.

AGROCOS

Digitanimal (2026-2027)

This project focuses on the development and technological transfer of a predictive livestock farming system for the estimation of soil organic carbon levels. The research aims to evolve an existing estimation model into a robust, interpretable, and operational tool by integrating soil analysis, livestock-related variables, and GPS-based animal monitoring data. The project also includes the iterative improvement of predictive algorithms, the development of a data visualization and decision-support platform, and the dissemination of scientific and technical results through collaboration activities, technical events, and knowledge transfer initiatives with Digitanimal.

TunAI

Satlink (2026-2027)

The TunAI project consists in the development of a system designed to predict the presence of tuna by analyzing historical catch data alongside various oceanographic conditions. This information is processed through an integrated analytical tool that simplifies complex datasets into practical operational products for use in maritime activities. To ensure the results are transparent and useful for decision-making, the project focuses on explainability, allowing users to see which environmental factors are driving the predictions. Additionally, the system includes a monitoring component to track performance and adjust the models as sea conditions change over time.

Research on GenAI applied to promotional campaigns in the retail sector

Tier1 & Optimization Research Group (US) (2025-2026)

This project explores the application of GenAI within the retail sector to automate and personalize promotional campaigns through a framework based on LLMs. Architectures based on RAG, intelligent agents and hybrid systems that combine prediction and generation to produce texts and dynamic coupons are analyzed. By analyzing historical purchase data and real-time behavior, the system enables dynamic audience segmentation and the automated generation of hyper-personalized content and omnichannel offers. Ultimately, the research aims to enhance key performance metrics such as conversion and ROI while ensuring rigorous standards for data privacy, explainability, and the ethical mitigation of algorithmic bias to achieve a scalable and responsible solution.

Pre-invoice estimation for Gas and Electricity contracts

Endesa (2025)

This project focuses on porting MATLAB code to Python to develop a system for estimating preliminary invoices for gas and electricity contracts. The goal is to create a more flexible, maintainable, and scalable solution for forecasting energy costs based on contract data and consumption patterns.

Plastic pollution from source to sea

Seureca Veolia (2025)

This project focuses on the development and deployment of an Open Data Platform to support data-driven decision-making around plastic management. Aligned with data governance guidelines and pilot initiatives, the platform will integrate contributions from voluntary data owners and serve as a Proof of Concept for Developing Member Countries. By offering tools for national and regional reporting, including KPIs and analytics, it aims to guide policies that promote a circular plastic value chain and prepare for future reporting obligations under the Global Plastic Treaty.

Weaving Connections

Seureca Veolia (2024-2025)

This project aims to map and consolidate data to create indicators that will assist in designing, evaluating, and improving public policies to combat energy poverty. It focuses on promoting the socio-economic development of vulnerable populations, including those in isolated or remote regions. The project involves mapping, extracting, transforming, and loading data to build a database of energy poverty indicators, which will be presented through an interactive dashboard.
