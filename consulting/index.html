Consulting projects

Estimación de prefactura para contratos de Gas y Electricidad

Endesa (2025)

This project focuses on porting MATLAB code to Python to develop a system for estimating preliminary invoices for gas and electricity contracts. The goal is to create a more flexible, maintainable, and scalable solution for forecasting energy costs based on contract data and consumption patterns.

Promoting action on plastic pollution from source to sea in Asia and the Pacific-Prioritizing and Implementing Actions to Reduce Marine Plastic Pollution

MRC Consultants and Transaction Advisers (2025)

This project focuses on the development and deployment of an Open Data Platform to support data-driven decision-making around plastic management. Aligned with data governance guidelines and pilot initiatives, the platform will integrate contributions from voluntary data owners and serve as a Proof of Concept for Developing Member Countries. By offering tools for national and regional reporting, including KPIs and analytics, it aims to guide policies that promote a circular plastic value chain and prepare for future reporting obligations under the Global Plastic Treaty.

AILIVE4ENV

Digitanimal (2024-2026)

AILIVE4ENV is a data-driven project focused on enhancing productivity and reducing the environmental impact of livestock farming through artificial intelligence, satellite imagery, and IoT data. By applying predictive and prescriptive models, it aims to deliver actionable recommendations for the sustainable management of natural resources, supporting the agri-livestock sector in its contribution to climate change mitigation.

Weaving Connections: A Comprehensive Diagnosis of Access to and Use of Electricity and Essential Services by Low-Income Population

MRC Consultants and Transaction Advisers (2024-2025)

This project aims to map and consolidate data to create indicators that will assist in designing, evaluating, and improving public policies to combat energy poverty. It focuses on promoting the socio-economic development of vulnerable populations, including those in isolated or remote regions. The project involves mapping, extracting, transforming, and loading data to build a database of energy poverty indicators, which will be presented through an interactive dashboard.

Geospatial mapping of least cost mini grid potential in Guatemala

MRC Consultants and Transaction Advisers (2020)

Data Science advisory activities for energy consulting projects financed by the World Bank to MRC Consultants, the consulting firm awarded the project. In this project, MRC will conduct a national electrification analysis in Guatemala, including the development of a geospatial electrification plan at minimal cost.
