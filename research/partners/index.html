Research partners

Centro de Investigación en Ciencias del Deporte (URJC)

CIDE Research Center at Rey Juan Carlos University focuses on high-impact research and innovation in sport sciences, leading over 56 projects and securing more than €3 million in external funding.

Visit CIDE

Tecnologías para el Análisis y Diagnóstico Territorial (URJC)

TADAT Research Group at Rey Juan Carlos University focuses on developing technologies for territorial analysis and environmental diagnosis. Their projects range from spatial AI for ecosystem monitoring to collaborations on protected areas and biosphere reserves at both national and European levels.

Academia Joven de España

Academia Joven de España aims to promote and support outstanding early-career researchers in Spain, while inspiring scientific vocations among younger generations across all fields of knowledge.

Visit AJE

Department of Industrial Engineering (UChile)

The Department of Industrial Engineering at the University of Chile, part of the Faculty of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, is a leading academic center in Latin America. Renowned for its excellence in research and education, the department trains top-tier industrial engineers—professionals with a strong foundation in science, mathematics, and business. With a distinguished faculty and a vibrant international graduate community, it stands out for its academic impact and the global success of its graduates.

Visit DII

Instituto de Investigación Biomédica de Salamanca

The Institute for Biomedical Research of Salamanca (IBSAL) is a leading center in Spain dedicated to biomedical research. Based in Salamanca, IBSAL integrates basic, clinical, translational, epidemiological, and health services research. It brings together top research efforts from the University Hospital of Salamanca, the Primary Care Management of Salamanca, and the biosciences departments at the University of Salamanca.

Visit IBSAL

Sistemas Complejos y Física Fundamental (URJC)

The SCFF Research Group at Rey Juan Carlos University focuses on the study, modeling, and prediction of complex systems and fundamental physics. Its interdisciplinary projects span from quantum fluid dynamics and Bayesian methods for security analysis to higher-order network structures and critical behavior in social and ecological systems.
