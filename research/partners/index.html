Research partners

At DSLAB, we collaborate with a wide network of research partners —⁠from industry leaders to academic groups and public institutions⁠— to drive innovation at the frontiers of data science. Some of these collaborations grew from consultancy relationships, while others were forged directly through shared research goals. This page highlights the organizations and teams that work with us to develop pioneering solutions and advance scientific knowledge together.

Academia Joven de España

Academia Joven de España aims to promote and support outstanding early-career researchers in Spain, while inspiring scientific vocations among younger generations across all fields of knowledge.

Visit AJE

AuditAI (UAH)

The AuditAI research group at the University of Alcalá specializes in the development and analysis of complex software systems. Their focus includes investigating high-quality software engineering practices to address challenges in cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and IoT. Additionally, the group conducts audits of AI developments, explores AI applications in programming environments, and advances innovation in AI technologies. This expertise aims to enhance productivity and meet the growing demands of modern technological industries.

Visit AuditAI

Centro de Investigación en Ciencias del Deporte (URJC)

The CIDE Research Center at Rey Juan Carlos University is a multidisciplinary center dedicated to advancing high-quality research and training in Sport Sciences. It focuses on developing innovative research, generating scientific knowledge, training new researchers in collaboration with national and international institutions, and promoting the transfer of research outcomes to society and the sports sector. Recognized as a High-Performance Research Group, CIDE contributes actively to fields such as Physical Activity and Health, Sports Performance, and Sports Education.

Visit CIDE

Centro de Investigación en Ciencias de Información Geoespacial

CentroGeo is a research center in Mexico dedicated to advancing geospatial information science. Its mission is to generate, apply, and share cutting-edge knowledge, train high-level specialists, and develop innovative technologies to address national, regional, and local challenges —⁠contributing to scientific and technological progress for the benefit of society.

Visit CentroGeo

DeNexus

DeNexus provides a full-stack cyber risk quantification and management platform specifically designed for industrial sectors such as energy, manufacturing, data centers, transportation, and critical infrastructures. Its solutions focus on environments with OT systems, helping organizations assess, manage, and mitigate cyber risks in complex industrial operations.

Learn more about DeNexus

Department of Industrial Engineering (UChile)

The Department of Industrial Engineering at the University of Chile, part of the Faculty of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, is a leading academic center in Latin America. Renowned for its excellence in research and education, the department trains top-tier industrial engineers —⁠professionals with a strong foundation in science, mathematics, and business. With a distinguished faculty and a vibrant international graduate community, it stands out for its academic impact and the global success of its graduates.

Visit DII

Ericsson

Ericsson is a Swedish multinational networking and telecommunications company. It provides services, software, and infrastructure in ICT for telecommunications operators. Its portfolio includes mobile and fixed broadband networks, IP networking equipment, operations and business support systems, as well as solutions for cable television, IPTV, video systems, and managed services.

Learn more about Ericsson

Grupo de Ingeniería de Medios (UEx)

The Media Engineering Group (GIM) at the University of Extremadura is made up of around thirty members and focuses on developing efficient and effective methods for extracting semantic content from digitally represented information. Their work aims to advance research and innovation in media engineering and related technologies.

Grupo de investigación en Aplicaciones Sociales e Interdisciplinares basadas en Agentes (UCM)

GRASIA is a research group at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid focused on designing and engineering sociotechnical systems to empower communities. Their work combines software engineering, artificial intelligence, data mining, sociology, and communication to address complex social challenges through technology-driven solutions.

Visit GRASIA

Indra

Indra is a global leader in technology and consulting, known for its strong commitment to innovation and the development of advanced solutions across key sectors such as aerospace, defense, mobility, and digital services. Headquartered in Spain, Indra plays a central role in large-scale technological and R&D projects worldwide, integrating cutting-edge research with practical applications.

Visit Indra

Instituto de Investigación Biomédica de Salamanca

The Institute for Biomedical Research of Salamanca (IBSAL) is a leading center in Spain dedicated to biomedical research. Based in Salamanca, IBSAL integrates basic, clinical, translational, epidemiological, and health services research. It brings together top research efforts from the University Hospital of Salamanca, the Primary Care Management of Salamanca, and the biosciences departments at the University of Salamanca.

Visit IBSAL

Interdisciplinary Mathematics Institute (UCM)

IMI at Complutense University of Madrid promotes research, collaboration, and innovation across mathematical disciplines. It connects mathematicians with scientists and professionals from other fields to solve complex problems through applied and theoretical mathematics.

Visit IMI

MedLab Media Group (now dezzai)

MedLab Media Group, now operating as Dezzai, is a technology company focused on developing advanced solutions for the healthcare sector. With a multidisciplinary team of over 60 professionals in IT, research, business, and journalism, the company applies artificial intelligence and data analytics to drive innovation and digital transformation in the global health ecosystem.

Learn more about dezzai

School of Science & Technology (IE)

The School of Science & Technology at IE University offers a practical and modern STEM education that prepares students for real-world challenges. With hands-on learning, strong faculty support, and connections to global companies and researchers, it helps students build the skills they need for successful careers in science and technology.

Visit CentroGeo

SensoWave

SensoWave is a trademark of MISC International S.L., created to develop innovative products and services in wireless technologies, IoT and Big Data. The company offers solutions across various sectors, including the livestock industry, eHealth, industrial applications, training systems for special forces and security agencies, and more.

Learn more about SensoWave

Sistemas Complejos y Física Fundamental (URJC)

The SCFF Research Group at Rey Juan Carlos University focuses on the study, modeling, and prediction of complex systems and fundamental physics. Its interdisciplinary projects span from quantum fluid dynamics and Bayesian methods for security analysis to higher-order network structures and critical behavior in social and ecological systems.

Visit SCFF

Tecnologías para el Análisis y Diagnóstico Territorial (URJC)

TADAT Research Group at Rey Juan Carlos University focuses on developing technologies for territorial analysis and environmental diagnosis. Their projects range from spatial AI for ecosystem monitoring to collaborations on protected areas and biosphere reserves at both national and European levels.
